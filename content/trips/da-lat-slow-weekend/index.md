Not every trip has to be a story about suffering. This one was the opposite: I took the
morning bus, dropped my bag at a tiny homestay and did nothing on purpose.

I spent most of Saturday in one café, watching the fog slide down the hill and fill the
valley like milk poured slowly into a bowl. Dinner was grilled rice paper at a street corner,
eaten standing up, with a stranger who gave me a full review of every stall on the street.

On Sunday I climbed Langbiang. It is more a long walk than a hike, and the top is crowded,
but the view makes you forget the crowd for a minute. Then the afternoon bus, a sleepy ride
home, and a note in my phone that says only: do this more often.

**Good to know.** Easy enough for anyone, and a good first trip to try with friends who are
not into long rides. Go on a weekday if you can, because weekends fill up fast.
